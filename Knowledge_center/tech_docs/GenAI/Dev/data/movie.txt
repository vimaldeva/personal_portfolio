# Dhurandhar Movie

**Dhurandhar** is a 2025 Hindi-language spy action thriller directed by Aditya Dhar. It stars Ranveer Singh in the lead, alongside Sanjay Dutt, R. Madhavan, Akshaye Khanna, and Arjun Rampal. The film was released in theatres on **December 5, 2025** and is described as a big-screen espionage drama with a gritty, high-stakes tone. [web:1][web:3][web:5]

## Storyline

The film is centered on an undercover spy mission that moves through Karachi’s criminal and intelligence networks. A mysterious operative infiltrates the underworld, rises through its ranks, and works to dismantle a terror-linked nexus from within. Another synopsis describes the central arc as involving RAW, secret operations, gangster networks, and a dangerous mission tied to Pakistan-based terror planning. [web:2][web:6]

A more detailed plot summary suggests that the lead character, Hamza, enters Pakistan, joins the gang of Rehman Dakait, and secretly passes intelligence back to RAW through an ally named Alam. The story also involves political tension, intelligence failures, and a larger operation called **Dhurandhar**. [web:2]

## Release And Viewing

The film was released in theatres on **December 5, 2025**. Later listings reported that it would be available on Netflix in multiple languages from **January 30, 2026**. [web:1][web:4][web:7]

## What Makes It Notable

Dhurandhar stands out because it combines espionage, organized crime, and political intrigue rather than following a simple action formula. The cast is unusually strong for a commercial thriller, with several established stars playing key supporting roles. The film is also linked with Aditya Dhar, who is known for intense, military-themed filmmaking. [web:3][web:5]

## Quick Take

If you want the shortest description, Dhurandhar is a high-budget Hindi spy thriller about an undercover mission inside a terror-and-gangster network, led by Ranveer Singh and backed by a major ensemble cast. Its release strategy also extended from theatres to streaming after the theatrical run. [web:2][web:7]
